# RT 1 Robotics Transformer for Real World Control

### RT 1 Robotics Transformer for Real World Control

## Summary
This paper introduces the Robotics Transformer (RT-1), a model designed to handle diverse robotic tasks efficiently. The model is trained on large-scale real-world data, demonstrating impressive generalization capabilities across various tasks and environments. It combines efficient architecture with high-capacity Transformers, enabling it to perform complex actions at 3 Hz despite its size of 35M parameters.

## Key Claims
- RT-1 can generalize well to new robotic tasks, environments, and objects.
- The model achieves over 97% success rate in performing up to 700 training instructions.
- It outperforms existing methods by 25%, 36%, and 18% on generalization metrics for distractors, backgrounds, and new scenarios.

## Source Evidence
The paper presents a detailed study of RT-1's performance using a large-scale dataset collected from real robots performing various tasks. The model was evaluated in both training and testing phases with over 130k demonstrations and 700 tasks. Results show significant improvements in generalization compared to previous methods.
