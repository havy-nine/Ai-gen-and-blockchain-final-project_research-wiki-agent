### PaLM-E: An Embodied Multimodal Language Model

## Summary
This paper introduces PaLM-E, an embodied multimodal language model that integrates real-world sensor modalities into large language models. It addresses grounding issues by directly incorporating visual and physical sensor data into language models for tasks such as robotic manipulation planning, visual question answering, and captioning.

## Key Claims
1. **Embodied Language Models**: PaLM-E is a single general-purpose multimodal language model that can handle embodied reasoning tasks, visual-language tasks, and language tasks.
2. **Transfer Learning**: The model benefits from diverse joint training across internet-scale domains including language, vision, and visual-language domains.
3. **Robotic Applications**: PaLM-E can address various robotic tasks using multi-modal sentences.

## Source Evidence
- The paper demonstrates the effectiveness of PaLM-E through a variety of embodied reasoning tasks, such as sorting colors into corners, grasping objects, and answering questions about robot environments.
- It shows that PaLM-E outperforms existing visual-language models in solving robotic reasoning tasks by directly incorporating sensor data.
